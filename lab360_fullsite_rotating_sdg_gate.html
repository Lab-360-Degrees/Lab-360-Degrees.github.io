Lab 360° Research

Science-first AI, from disorder to design

Our R&D combines large-scale simulation on high-performance computing with statistical and machine-learning methods to build explainable models of self-organisation and failure in living and artificial systems.

🧊

Soft, glassy & active matter

Transitions between fluid-like and rigid states in disordered systems, the physics behind our predictive models.

🌊

Flow, jamming & living machines

How driven and active systems flow, jam and reorganise, informing swarm and collective AI.

🧪

Material failure & molecular information

How molecular systems store, process and relay information.

🌐

Catastrophe science & emergent intelligence

From tissue dynamics to critical cascades in networks and economies.

Six research directions

Each direction feeds a reusable modelling engine behind our products.

Direction 01

Molecular information processing

Decoding how molecular systems store and relay information to engineer more responsive artificial systems.

Direction 02

Cellular migration & turbulence

Collective dynamics of migrating cells in tissue formation, repair and failure.

Direction 03

Failure & jamming of amorphous systems

Predictive models of resilience, critical points and jamming in disordered materials.

Direction 04

Predicting vulnerability & avalanches

Critical cascades, from natural avalanches to interconnected economies.

Direction 05

Autonomous & critical phenomena

Self-organised behaviour at critical thresholds, balancing stability and adaptability.

Direction 06

Generative physical intelligence

Generative models integrated with physical laws to produce physically plausible dynamics and structures.
